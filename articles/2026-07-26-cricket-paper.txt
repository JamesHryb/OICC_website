Title: Old Imperials CC Featured in the Cricket Paper and Wisden
Category: General
We’ve been featured in The Cricket Paper, the UK’s leading weekly Cricket magazine. It’s a well-known source for international and domestic Cricket News, with its feature writers being the likes of Mark Butcher, Derek Pringle, and some of the team from Wisden.

There’s an article worringly early in their latest edition (right after the India England series rundown), covering our tour to Mongolia, plus  the story about club’s journey so far. It’s all happened within the space of a week, so haven’t been able to say much about it!

The print version should be available in all good newsagents, in general this covers supermarkets and bookshops!

The article has also been reposted to Wisden's own website!
